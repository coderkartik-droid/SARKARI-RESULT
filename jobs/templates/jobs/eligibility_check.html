How eligibility is evaluated

  • 1
    Date of birth. Your date of birth must fall inside the exam’s eligible date-of-birth window. Both boundaries are inclusive.
  • 2
    Qualification. Your qualification must match at least one qualification accepted for the exam. A higher qualification does not automatically satisfy a different one.
  • 3
    Unverified. Exams with criteria that cannot be checked are shown separately as unverified rather than eligible.
  • 4
    Official rules. Category relaxations (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) and special conditions should always be confirmed in the official notification.
